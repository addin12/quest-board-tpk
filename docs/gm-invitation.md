# Invitation to Game Masters

Messages to send to Game Masters (WhatsApp, Discord, a group), in English and Indonesian. Written for people who already run games and have never heard of
the site. Every claim is true of the live site on 2026-11-03: free, no bookings or payments, an email sign-in link, an admin review before a listing shows,
and the email address is never published. Change the link if the site moves to its own domain.

Links: <https://quest-board-tpk.pages.dev/en/list-your-game/> (English), <https://quest-board-tpk.pages.dev/id/list-your-game/> (Indonesian).

## Short, for a chat or a DM

**English**

> Hi! The Pandemonium Kollektive now has a free board where players in Jabodetabek and online can find tabletop RPG games. If you run games, you can list yours:
> https://quest-board-tpk.pages.dev/en/list-your-game/
> It takes about ten minutes. You sign in with your email (no password), fill in a short profile and your game, and an admin approves it. Players then message you
> directly on WhatsApp. No bookings, no payments, no fees. Questions? Just reply here.

**Indonesian**

> Halo! The Pandemonium Kollektive punya papan game TTRPG gratis untuk pemain di Jabodetabek dan online. Kalau kamu memandu game, kamu bisa memasang game-mu di sini:
> https://quest-board-tpk.pages.dev/id/list-your-game/
> Cuma sekitar sepuluh menit. Masuk pakai email (tanpa password), isi profil singkat dan game-mu, lalu admin menyetujuinya. Setelah itu pemain menghubungimu langsung
> lewat WhatsApp. Tanpa booking, tanpa pembayaran, tanpa biaya. Ada pertanyaan? Balas saja pesan ini.

## Longer, for a group or a post

**English**

> **List your game on Quest Board**
>
> Quest Board is a free noticeboard for tabletop RPG games, run by The Pandemonium Kollektive. Players browse by game system, genre, play style and mechanic,
> then message the Game Master directly. The site takes no bookings and no money: you agree on seats and price with your players yourself.
>
> What you do:
> 1. Open https://quest-board-tpk.pages.dev/en/list-your-game/ and sign in with your email. We send you a link; there is no password.
> 2. Fill in your profile once: display name, a short bio, your city, your WhatsApp number. Discord, Instagram and a photo are optional.
> 3. Add your game: system, schedule, where (online or a public place such as a cafe), price per seat, content warnings and safety tools, and a cover picture if you have one.
> 4. An admin checks it. When it is approved, it appears on the site and you get an email.
>
> Your email address is never shown. Your WhatsApp number is shown only so players can reach you. You can change, mark full or remove a listing any time, and a
> listing leaves the site on its own after the session date.

**Indonesian**

> **Pasang game-mu di Quest Board**
>
> Quest Board adalah papan pengumuman gratis untuk game TTRPG, dijalankan The Pandemonium Kollektive. Pemain mencari berdasarkan sistem game, genre, gaya bermain
> dan mekanik, lalu menghubungi Game Master langsung. Situs ini tidak mengurus booking atau uang: soal kursi dan harga, kamu sepakati sendiri dengan pemain.
>
> Caranya:
> 1. Buka https://quest-board-tpk.pages.dev/id/list-your-game/ dan masuk dengan emailmu. Kami kirim tautan; tidak ada password.
> 2. Isi profilmu sekali saja: nama tampilan, bio singkat, kota, nomor WhatsApp. Discord, Instagram, dan foto itu opsional.
> 3. Tambahkan game-mu: sistem, jadwal, tempat (online atau tempat umum seperti kafe), harga per kursi, peringatan konten dan alat keselamatan, serta gambar sampul kalau ada.
> 4. Admin memeriksanya. Setelah disetujui, game-mu tampil di situs dan kamu dapat email.
>
> Alamat emailmu tidak pernah ditampilkan. Nomor WhatsApp hanya ditampilkan supaya pemain bisa menghubungimu. Kamu bisa mengubah, menandai penuh, atau menghapus
> game kapan saja, dan game hilang dari situs sendiri setelah tanggal sesinya.

## Before you send it

- Test the whole flow once yourself first (sign in, add a profile, add a game, approve it on `/en/admin/`): nobody has done it on the live site yet.
- The Indonesian text is the agent's wording; read it as a native speaker before it goes out (see [indonesian-review.md](indonesian-review.md) for the site's own strings).
